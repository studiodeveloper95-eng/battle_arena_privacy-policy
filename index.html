Privacy Policy

Battle Arena – Esports Tournaments PK
Effective Date: 6 July 2026

Battle Arena – Esports Tournaments PK (“we”, “our”, “us”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our mobile application, Battle Arena – Tournaments (the “App”).

This application is intended only for users located in Pakistan.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Picture (from Google Sign-In or uploaded by you)
  • WhatsApp / Mobile Number
  • In-Game Name, In-Game UID and Team Name
  • Payment Screenshot or Receipt (uploaded for payment verification purposes)
  • Mobile wallet account details (EasyPaisa / JazzCash) provided by you for prize withdrawals

b) Authentication Data

We use Firebase Authentication for logging in with Email/Password or Google Sign-In. Your passwords are securely handled by Firebase and are never accessible to us.

c) App Usage Data

  • Tournament registrations
  • Leaderboard performance
  • App interaction data to help improve functionality and security
  • Crash logs and diagnostics (via Firebase Crashlytics)

d) Advertising Data

  • Device Advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device information and ad interaction data (views, clicks)

This data is collected by our advertising partner, Google AdMob, as described in the Advertising section below.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Register users in tournaments
  • Verify entry fee payments and process prize withdrawals
  • Display leaderboards and prize results
  • Send notifications and updates (via Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • Display advertisements (via Google AdMob)
  • Improve app functionality and security

3. Payments

We do not process card payments inside the App and never collect bank card information. Deposits are made externally via EasyPaisa or JazzCash, and users upload a payment receipt screenshot for verification purposes. For prize withdrawals, users provide their mobile wallet account details (EasyPaisa / JazzCash account number), which we store securely and use only to process payouts. Payment receipts are retained only as long as needed for verification and dispute resolution.

4. Data Security

All user data is stored securely using Firebase services and encrypted in transit. We implement reasonable safeguards, but no system is 100% secure.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share limited data (Advertising ID, approximate location and device information) with Google AdMob solely for the purpose of serving advertisements, as described in the Advertising section. Beyond that, we may share data only when required by law or to provide core App functionality.

6. User Rights

  • Access and update your profile
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Log out anytime

You can delete your account directly within the App via the "Delete Account" option in the menu drawer. Alternatively, you can contact us at 📧 studio.developer95@gmail.com for assistance.

7. Age Requirement

The App features tournaments with entry fees and cash prizes and is intended only for users aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that a user is under 18, we will remove the account and associated data.

8. Notifications

We may send push notifications related to tournaments, updates, and announcements using Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).

9. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to support the functionality of the App. These services include:

  • Firebase Authentication – for login and account management
  • Firebase Firestore – for storing user and tournament data
  • Firebase Storage – for storing profile images and payment receipts
  • Firebase Remote Config – for delivering app configuration
  • Firebase Analytics – for tracking user behavior and improving the app
  • Firebase Crashlytics – for monitoring app crashes and performance
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) – for sending notifications
  • Google Sign-In – for authentication via Google
  • Google AdMob – for displaying advertisements
  • WhatsApp & YouTube – for external links (e.g., tournament group links, community channels, instructional videos)

10. Advertising

The App displays advertisements served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • Your device's Advertising ID
  • Your IP address and approximate (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device information and ad interaction data (views and clicks)

This data is used for ad serving, frequency capping, ad measurement, and fraud prevention. Google processes this data in accordance with its own privacy policy. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any changes will be effective immediately upon posting. The effective date of the updated policy will be reflected at the top of this page.

12. Force App Updates

To ensure your app stays up to date with new features and security updates, Battle Arena – Tournaments may require mandatory app updates to continue usage. Users will be notified when an update is available.
